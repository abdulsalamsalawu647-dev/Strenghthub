MEET THE ATHLETES
OUR ATHLETES

Built Through Training.
Driven By Progress.

Aurex athletes come from different areas of fitness and movement. From calisthenics and strength training to gymnastics, acrobatics and martial arts, each athlete brings their own discipline and training journey.

Aurex is built around progression. Our athletes represent different ways of training while sharing the same focus: putting in the work, developing their skills and becoming stronger over time.

THE ATHLETES

Aurex athletes train across different disciplines, bringing together strength, movement, skill and athletic performance.
